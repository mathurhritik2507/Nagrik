## Full description
Small actions can help build stronger communities.

Nagrik is an independent civic participation app designed to help people in India take constructive steps in their neighborhoods and keep track of their own efforts.

With Nagrik you can:
- Record civic activities, including cleanliness and volunteering efforts.
- Save notes about local civic issues, along with optional photos and location descriptions.
- Explore civic learning guidance and practical steps for responsible participation.
- Complete challenges and view your personal progress with virtual XP.

**Important:** Civic actions and issue notes saved in the Android app are a personal record. They are **not automatically submitted to a municipal authority or government office**. Nagrik is not a government app, has no government affiliation, and does not guarantee that a reported issue will be reviewed or resolved.

Virtual XP and badges are participation indicators, **not cash or redeemable rewards**.

Nagrik is in beta. Features may differ from the website, and feedback helps us improve the experience.
